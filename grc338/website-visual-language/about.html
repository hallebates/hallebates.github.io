About Us

Hightide Cafe is a small, coastal-inspired coffee company founded in 2026 by Halle Bates. Created from a love of both the beach and great coffee, Hightide was born from the idea that coffee should feel just as refreshing and meaningful as a day spent by the ocean.

Halle started Hightide Cafe to combine her passion for coastal living with her appreciation for thoughtfully made drinks and food. Every recipe is developed in-house and crafted with care, using responsibly sourced coffee beans and high-quality ingredients. Our menu features handcrafted espresso drinks, freshly brewed coffee, and simple food options designed to complement a relaxed, beachside lifestyle.

At Hightide, we believe that great coffee is about more than just flavor; it’s about connection, creativity, and community. Through our work, we strive to create a space that reflects the easygoing spirit of the coast while supporting sustainable practices and honest craftsmanship. We’re proud to serve our community with intention, quality, and a shared love for the ocean and the coffee that fuels our days.

Looking ahead, Hightide Cafe aims to grow into a welcoming neighborhood gathering place where locals and visitors alike can slow down and enjoy quality coffee in a comfortable, coastal atmosphere. By continuing to innovate our menu, partner with responsible suppliers, and engage with the surrounding community, we hope to build a brand that reflects authenticity, environmental awareness, and the laid-back spirit that inspired Hightide from the very beginning.
