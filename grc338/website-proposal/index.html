Introduction

Hightide Coffee

High Tide Coffee is a small, coastal-inspired coffee company that serves handcrafted espresso drinks, freshly brewed coffee, and a selection of fresh, made-to-order breakfast and snack items. Each beverage and food item is prepared with care, using responsibly sourced ingredients and attention to quality, consistency, and flavor. The company offers a rotating menu of classic and seasonal drinks, along with pastries, breakfast sandwiches, and light bites.

About

[Picture from the Grand Opening.]

High Tide Coffee is a small, coastal-inspired coffee company founded in 2026 by Halle Bates. Created from a love of both the beach and great coffee, High Tide Coffee was born from the idea that coffee should feel just as refreshing and meaningful as a day spent by the ocean.

Halle started High Tide Coffee to combine her passion for coastal living with her appreciation for thoughtfully made drinks and food. Every recipe is developed in-house and crafted with care, using responsibly sourced coffee beans and high-quality ingredients. Our menu features handcrafted espresso drinks, freshly brewed coffee, and simple food options designed to complement a relaxed, beachside lifestyle.

At High Tide Coffee, we believe that great coffee is about more than just flavor;it’s about connection, creativity, and community. Through our work, we strive to create a space that reflects the easygoing spirit of the coast while supporting sustainable practices and honest craftsmanship. We’re proud to serve our community with intention, quality, and a shared love for the ocean and the coffee that fuels our days.
